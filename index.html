01 / About

Building a career beyond borders

Fatik Rahman is an Indian entrepreneur, Top Rated Plus Upwork Freelancer, Upwork Coach, Freelancing Mentor, Web Developer, and Digital Business Strategist.

He began his professional journey while continuing his education and developed an early interest in websites, technology, and online business. What began as curiosity gradually became a full-time freelancing career.

Over the years, he has worked with startups, small businesses, entrepreneurs, and established organisations. His experience includes web development, digital platforms, client acquisition, project management, remote team collaboration, online entrepreneurship, and professional mentoring.

Today, he helps businesses strengthen their digital presence while guiding aspiring freelancers toward building practical and sustainable careers.
